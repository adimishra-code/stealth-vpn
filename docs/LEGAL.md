## 2. Privacy Policy

### What We Collect
- Email address (for account management)
- Payment transaction records (required for tax compliance)
- Device names (user-provided, for device identification)
- Aggregated per-device bandwidth (no per-site breakdown)
- VPN server node and IP (for load balancing)

### What We Do NOT Collect
- Browsing history
- DNS request logs
- Connection destination IPs
- Timestamps of individual connections
- Content of transmitted data
- Installation identifiers beyond WG wireguard key

### Data Retention
- Account data: active until account deletion
- Bandwidth aggregate: 90-day roll bounding
- Auth logs: 7 days, then deleted
- Payment records: 7 per Indian Tax Act 1961

### GDPR Compliance (EU users)
- Right to access: request you data, us zip.
- Request delete: full account removal.
